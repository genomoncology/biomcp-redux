BioMCP <Fact k="version" /> resolves genes against HGNC and reads gene sections from MyGene.info, CIViC, ClinGen, gnomAD, the Human Protein Atlas, Reactome and OncoKB. Proteins come from UniProt, InterPro and Complex Portal. Pathways come from Reactome and WikiPathways. This page follows BRAF from gene to protein to pathway.

## Requirements

- BioMCP installed. No key is needed except for the `oncokb` section.

## 1. Read the gene card

The [quickstart](/quickstart/#3-get-one-record) reads the BRAF card. `get gene` takes a symbol, an alias, a previous symbol, an HGNC, NCBI Gene or Ensembl identifier, or a cytogenetic band, and up to 20 inputs at once. `biomcp search gene` searches names and filters by `--locus-group` or `--band`.

| Section | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `protein` | UniProt reviewed human proteins |
| `civic` | CIViC gene curation |
| `clingen` | ClinGen validity and dosage |
| `constraint` | gnomAD constraint |
| `expression` | Human Protein Atlas |
| `pathways` | Reactome |
| `oncokb` | OncoKB, with your key |
| `all` | every section above |

## 3. Read the protein

`get protein` takes a UniProt accession. `biomcp search protein BRAF` finds it from a name.

<Example id="protein-get" />

Protein sections are `features`, `domains`, `isoforms`, `complexes`, `structures`, `function`, `sequence` and `all`. Each also has a relation command for paging, such as `biomcp protein domains P15056`. [How BioMCP works](/how-biomcp-works/#relation-commands-read-collections) shows its output.

## 4. Find a pathway and its members

<Example id="pathway-search" />

`pathway genes` lists a pathway's participants. Gene products carry their HGNC identifier when the source maps one exactly; other participants, such as a small molecule, keep their native reference:

<Example id="pathway-genes" />

WikiPathways reads from a local bundle; run `biomcp sync wikipathways` once. `pathway events` lists a Reactome pathway's events.

## Verify

`get gene BRAF clingen` returns ClinGen rows with state `data`, and `get protein P15056` returns `BRAF_HUMAN`.

## Limits

Sections report each source's curation as published. A ClinGen classification names its disease and date. Pathway membership is the source's; BioMCP runs no enrichment analysis and infers no gene function.
