## Why it works

EMA publishes its medicines table as a download. `biomcp sync ema` stores it, and reads answer from the stored copy. The `regulatory` section returns EMA's own status and dated events.

## Limits

The answer is as fresh as your last sync. Run `biomcp sync ema` again for current data. EMA's status covers the European Union only.
