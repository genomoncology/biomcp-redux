Add `--lat=`, `--lon=` and `--distance=` with units to `biomcp search trial`. Write each with an equals sign: a negative longitude such as `-74.0060` is read as an option otherwise, and the command stops with `usage.arguments`. This BioMCP 1.0 run finds recruiting melanoma trials with a site within 50 miles of New York City.

## Why it works

ClinicalTrials.gov filters by the distance from the point to each trial site. `--distance` needs explicit units, `mi` or `km`. The search returns whole trials; `biomcp get trial NCT locations` lists each site with its own status.

## Limits

A trial matches when any of its sites is in range; that site may not be recruiting. `--facility` cannot combine with a radius. Distances are the registry's calculation from site coordinates.
