Combine `--has-results` with `--count-only`. BioMCP returns ClinicalTrials.gov's own total without rows. This BioMCP 1.0 run counts melanoma trials with posted results.

## Command

<Example id="a-trials-results" />

## Why it works

`--has-results` keeps trials with results posted to the registry. `--count-only` asks the registry for its total and returns it as the source's `count`.

## Limits

Posted results are those on ClinicalTrials.gov. Results published only in journals are not counted. The total changes as the registry updates.
