BioMCP <Fact k="version" /> entity `disease`. A disease term from MONDO, DOID or NCIt, or a SEER cancer site. Search resolves words to ontology terms. Get reads one exact identifier.

## Sections of get disease

`synonyms`, `xrefs`, `hierarchy`, `genes`, `phenotypes`, `incidence`, `survival`. The [get disease reference](/reference/commands/get-disease/) describes each one.
