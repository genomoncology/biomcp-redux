BioMCP <Fact k="version" />, the command-line tool and MCP server for biomedical data, groups its commands by [entity](/reference/entities/), the kind of record they read. This page covers `phenotype`. A Human Phenotype Ontology term, with its disease and gene associations from the Monarch Initiative. Terms are terminology. Associations are the publisher's assertions, not diagnoses.
