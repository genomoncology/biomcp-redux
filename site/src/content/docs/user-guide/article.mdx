BioMCP <Fact k="version" />, the command-line tool and MCP server for biomedical data, groups its commands by [entity](/reference/entities/), the kind of record they read. This page covers `article`. A published paper, read by PMID, PMCID or DOI. Sections add full text, supplements, annotations, citation counts, a summary, indexing and the PDF link.

## Sections of get article

`annotations`, `fulltext`, `supplements`, `tldr`, `citations`, `indexing`, `pdf`, `all`. The [get article reference](/reference/commands/get-article/) describes each one.
