BioMCP <Fact k="version" /> has four entities for drugs and their safety: `drug`, `adverse-event`, `pgx` and `diagnostic`. Each source has its own identifiers, so you search one source, take its identifier, and read that source's record. This page follows vemurafenib, a BRAF inhibitor, through several sources.

## Requirements

- BioMCP installed. No key is needed. An openFDA key raises openFDA's rate limit.
- DDInter, EMA, WHO and the FDA companion diagnostics list read local bundles. Run `biomcp sync ddinter` once before step 5; see [local data](/local-data/).

## 1. Search a drug

MyChem.info is the default drug source:

## 5. List interaction partners

DDInter returns partners and its interaction level, five rows per page by default:

<Example id="drug-interactions" />

`total` is DDInter's count for this drug. `--section-limit` and `--section-offset` page the rest.

## 6. Search adverse event reports

FAERS is the default adverse event source. The columns are the report ID, the report's seriousness flag (`1` serious, `2` not serious) and the date FDA first received it:

<Example id="ae-search" />

`--reaction`, `--serious death`, `--serious hospitalization`, `--received-from` and `--received-to` filter the reports. `--source maude` searches medical device reports. `--source vaers` reads CDC WONDER report summaries for `MMR`, `COVID19` or one exact influenza code; `search adverse-event influenza --source vaers` lists the influenza codes. No VAERS reply appears on this site: CDC WONDER has returned `bad_reply` for MMR and `deadline_exceeded` for COVID19, and the [VAERS source page](/sources/vaers/) shows the command to try. `get adverse-event ID` reads one report with sections such as `drugs` and `reactions`. [Find FDA adverse event reports for a drug](/how-to/faers-reports/) counts reports under each filter.

## 7. Read pharmacogenomic guidelines

CPIC lists the guidelines for a gene and the drugs each covers:

<Example id="pgx-get" />

The `recommendations` section returns CPIC's recommendation rows, and `diplotype` looks up a declared diplotype. `--source clinpgx` with `annotations --kind clinical|guideline|label` reads ClinPGx annotations. [Find CPIC guidelines and gene-drug pairs for a gene](/how-to/pgx-guidelines/) adds CPIC levels and a diplotype lookup.

## 8. Find companion diagnostics

The FDA companion diagnostics list matches a biomarker word in its rows:

<Example id="diagnostic-cdx" />

The list has one row per device, indication and drug, so a device appears once per row. `--source gtr` searches the Genetic Testing Registry, and `--source who-ivd` searches WHO prequalified diagnostics. `get diagnostic P-NUMBER --source fda-pma` and `get diagnostic K-NUMBER --source fda-510k` read FDA device records. [Find FDA companion diagnostics for a biomarker](/how-to/companion-diagnostics/) walks through one biomarker.

## Verify

Each step returns status `complete` with its source in state `ok`. The label card's `next` list names `indications_and_usage`.

## Limits

A report count is a count of reports. It does not show that a drug caused an event, and it cannot measure risk, incidence or compare drugs. A label, a guideline or a companion diagnostic row is the source's text, not a treatment recommendation. BioMCP calculates no dose, phenotype or activity score.
