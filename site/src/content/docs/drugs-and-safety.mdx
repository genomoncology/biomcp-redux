BioMCP <Fact k="version" /> has four [entities](/reference/entities/) for drugs and their safety: `drug`, `adverse-event`, `pgx` and `diagnostic`. Each source has its own identifiers, so you search one source, take its identifier, and read that source's record. This page follows vemurafenib, a BRAF inhibitor, through its identity, targets and FDA approval record, and links a page for each further task.

## Requirements

- BioMCP installed. No key is needed. An openFDA key raises openFDA's rate limit.
- EMA and WHO read local [bundles](/local-data/). Run `biomcp sync ema` or `biomcp sync who-pq` before step 1 searches those sources. Before the sync the reply is `failed`, the source's state is `syncing`, the exit code is 4, and `next` names the sync.

## Verify

Each step returns status `complete` with its source in state `ok`. Drugs@FDA lists application NDA202429.

## Limits

A report count is a count of reports. It does not show that a drug caused an event, and it cannot measure risk, incidence or compare drugs. A label, a guideline or a companion diagnostic row is the source's text, not a treatment recommendation. BioMCP calculates no dose, phenotype or activity score.
