# 0217 — Search phenotype terms in Monarch and get the exact HPO and Monarch term cards

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 4

Depends on: 0211
Depends on: 0214

## Outcome

`search phenotype TEXT` sends the literal text to the Monarch search endpoint that j14210 captured, restricted at the provider to `namespace=HP` and the phenotype category, and returns a five-header page in native order with each header's HP ID, label and native match score, matched field and match type, the source total, and `--limit` and `--offset` paging natively. `get phenotype HP:ID --source hpo` returns the HPO term card from the exact term request j14227 captured, with the complete definition, comment and publication references. `get phenotype HP:ID --source monarch` returns the independent Monarch term card from the exact entity request, with the complete description, association counts by scope and the deprecation fact if 0208 proved it. Each card lists the sections its source offers. The existing retirement of every `search phenotype` request narrows to the HP ID set form, so literal text reaches the new command.

- Why: the first phenotype question asks what a feature is called, and the PM rulings keep HP-namespace Monarch term search and exact HPO and Monarch term cards.
- Starts from: phenotype.md's command table, native candidate findings, exact card findings and measured cost table, with the Phenotype rulings in `2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md`; the j14204, j14210 and j14227 captures and 0208's middle page and obsolete card witnesses; `crates/biomcp-adapters/src/monarch/` and the `search phenotype` arm in `crates/biomcp/src/retired/disease.rs`; the shared transport and adapter shape in `architecture.md`; 0211's types and 0214's registry.
- Rules: no first candidate is selected, and no phrase is rewritten, split or expanded. A comma in the text stays part of the literal query. A returned item outside the HP namespace makes the reply a failed outcome with a problem and is never reclassified locally. The reply's limit and offset must match the request. No local rerank or score blend. A card whose returned ID differs from the requested HP ID is dropped with a problem and never cached. No card merges or falls back to the other source. The complete definition and comment are never shortened to meet a budget. An obsolete replacement action exists only where 0208's card supplies a replacement. Next steps follow architecture 6.10: each header gets one exact get step, then the page step, then at most three other steps. Each search header's get step is `get phenotype HP:ID` with the default `hpo` source, per the 2026-10-26 PM ruling. PM ruling 2026-10-26: `get phenotype HP:ID` defaults to `hpo` for the card, `synonyms` and `xrefs`; `--source monarch` selects the Monarch card and its versions of those sections. `hierarchy`, `diseases` and `genes` exist only in Monarch, so they use Monarch without a flag, and naming `--source hpo` with them fails before I/O. Help is never a step. A disabled source supplies no workaround provider.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Cases replay the j14210 and j14227 replies and 0208's witnesses. A case whose exact request those jobs did not send gets one bounded keyless pm job capture with zero retries before adapter code. P02 and P05 assert the exact label first; P01 asserts `HP:0000957` second behind `HP:0005605` with no selection; P06, P10 and P14 assert subtype and multiplicity stay separate headers; P08 asserts `HP:0001263` first with no replacement of the phrase; P09 asserts Monarch's successful empty reply; P11 asserts the obsolete `HP:0001676` row stays in native order; P16 asserts the 355 total; P24 asserts the unknown string's successful empty reply. 0208's pages assert first, middle and terminal paging with exact next arguments. All eighteen exact HPO cards assert their returned identity, and the clinical reviewer's retained cases assert the complete Global developmental delay definition with its age restriction and comment, Macrocephaly's measurement definition, the Pancreatic cysts pseudocyst comment and the pulmonary cyst comments. P16 asserts the Monarch Seizure card, and `HP:0001676` asserts the deprecation fact if 0208 proved it. `HP:9999999` asserts an absent term distinct from a failure. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases assert a failed outcome with source and terms, never an empty page. The job measures each command in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design ceilings of 1,418 comparable bytes for a search page, 1,612 for an HPO card and 696 for the Monarch card, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in phenotype.md. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
