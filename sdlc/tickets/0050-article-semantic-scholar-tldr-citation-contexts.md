# 0050 — Add the tldr section and Semantic Scholar citation contexts

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get article <input> tldr` returns Semantic Scholar's machine-written one-sentence summary with its model and Semantic Scholar's citation and influential citation counts, and `article citations <ID> --source semantic-scholar` and `article references <ID> --source semantic-scholar` page Semantic Scholar's citing and cited papers in its own order, each with intents, the influential flag and the first citation context with the total, with or without the user's key, while iCite stays the default source for both, through the CLI and MCP.
