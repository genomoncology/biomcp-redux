## Outcome

`get phenotype HP:ID diseases --source monarch` sends the Monarch association request with the HP ID as object, the disease subject category and `direct=true`, and returns source disease association rows in native order. `get phenotype HP:ID genes --source monarch` sends the same request with the gene subject category and the human subject taxon `NCBITaxon:9606`, and returns source human gene association rows. Each row reuses the disease slice's `DiseaseAssociation` record and the existing Monarch row mapping, with the requested HP ID beside the actual subject and object, original IDs, predicate, evidence, publications, provenance, negation, qualifiers, closures and the native subject taxon. Each page names its `direct` scope, carries the native association total, defaults to one complete row and pages natively with `--section-limit` and `--section-offset`. The Monarch card from 0217 gains both section steps.

- Why: the PM rulings keep `diseases` and `genes` sections that reuse the disease slice's association model, and the reverse Seizure captures show the publisher's direct scope and human gene rows.
- Starts from: phenotype.md's exact cards and association identity table, the Monarch association row of the source fields table and the association acceptance cases, with the Phenotype rulings in `2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md`; the j14227 reverse Seizure and P18 to P23 pages and 0208's next page and wrong-taxon witnesses; `crates/biomcp-adapters/src/monarch/` and its `associations` function; 0142 as the pattern; 0211's types and 0217's dispatch.
- Rules: there is one association representation. A row whose object differs from the requested HP ID, or a gene row whose subject taxon is not `NCBITaxon:9606`, makes the reply a bad reply with a problem, following the existing Monarch check, and it never enters the cache or a silently filtered page. No model organism or ortholog row is included. Two rows for one term with different provenance never collapse. Null evidence or negation stays absent, and explicit false stays false. PM ruling 2026-10-26: 0240 may be accepted while the shared `DiseaseAssociation` model still prints `evidence: []` for null Monarch evidence; 0253 repairs that field for disease and phenotype rows, and the 0240 synthetic direct-pages case keeps its current output until then. No qualifier or publication moves between rows or between a subtype and its parent. A total counts association rows, not genes, diseases or patients. A gene association is never a causal clinical statement. The existing `get disease MONDO:ID phenotypes --source monarch` keeps its closure-expanded contract unchanged, and this ticket adds no second route to syndrome annotations. Disabling `monarch`, `hpo` or `orphanet` skips both sections before any request, cache read or replay, and `restricted` alone leaves them available. Next steps follow architecture 6.10's association rule: one exact pivot per distinct linked disease or gene in native order, offered only where the existing disease or gene command accepts the returned identifier, then the page step, then at most three other steps. No HGNC symbol becomes an identity and no NCBIGene ID is relabeled. The design maps P21 to P23 to this section, but their captures are gene-keyed pages, so they serve as row mapping fixtures for the subject taxon, the original NCBIGene identity and NF1's PMID:8317503 on its second row only, and this ticket adds no gene-keyed route.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Cases replay the j14227 replies and 0208's witnesses.
