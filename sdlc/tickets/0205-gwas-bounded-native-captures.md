## Outcome

One bounded keyless pm job captures the GWAS Catalog v2 witnesses that `planning/slices/gwas.md` and the GWAS rulings in `decisions/2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md` still need before the GWAS types are accepted, and gwas.md records each one as proven, empty or failed. The witnesses are: a known-positive literal reported-trait text search; a known-positive association collection for one exact study accession, such as GCST000035; native p-value-ascending order for each of the variant, trait text, trait ID, gene and study selectors and for the captured gene plus trait ID conjunction, beside the order already proven for the PMID selector; a second source page under that order for one selector other than PMID; a negative boundary for each selector, such as a well-formed accession or ontology ID with no source records; a p-value threshold that crosses a source page boundary, chosen from rows of one captured selector so that every row on its first page qualifies and a later page holds the first row above the threshold; and one association whose p-value mantissa and exponent fall outside the finite double range, or a recorded search that found none. For each reply the ledger also records whether the self link echoes every sent selector, because the adapter checks request identity from it. Raw replies, headers, the ledger and the recipe go to the evidence store, and no product code changes.

- Why: the slice rules require a captured known-positive case for every selector and sort before implementation enables it, and the 0193 captures left the trait text, study collection, per-selector order, a non-PMID second page and a threshold crossing a page unproven.
- Starts from: gwas.md's measured public-tier behavior, fields proved by capture and outside-in case plan, with the GWAS rulings, which win over the design text; the j14205, j14220, j14229, j14240, j14247 and j14249 archives and the captured OpenAPI document for the native parameter names, including the singular `show_child_trait` and the extended-gene-set parameter; `crates/biomcp-adapters/src/gwas/`.
- Rules: the recipe names each exact v2 URL before the job runs, sends each row once in order with one second between admissions, a 20-second deadline and a 3 MiB body bound, and refuses redirects and retries. No altered query, fallback host, v1 path or extra page. A 429 or 5xx stops the source. No credential, summary-statistics file or bundle body. No row is chosen by local ontology or gene-name inference, and EFO_0000305 is never rewritten to MONDO_0004989. A failed or empty witness keeps its capability deferred: without a trait text positive, `--trait` stays unsupported; without the study collection, `--study` and the study `associations` section stay deferred; without native order for a selector, that selector stays deferred, because the ruling fixes p-value-ascending order as the default; without the threshold crossing, 0215 proves its page walk with synthetic replies only. Any deferred selector is reported to the PM through pm progress before the types ticket starts. No clinical answer is calculated from a capture.
- Proof: the job ledger records every attempt with its exact URL, status, bytes, SHA-256 and timing, and the archive path and hash appear in gwas.md beside each witness's result, and through pm progress. A reviewer checks that each witness's result names the capability it unlocks or defers. Source replies stay outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
