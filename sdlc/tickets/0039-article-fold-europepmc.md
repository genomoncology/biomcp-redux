# 0039 — Fold named sources, starting with Europe PMC

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`search article --source pubmed,europepmc` runs each named source in parallel with its own deadline, removes duplicates by identifier, shows on each item which sources found it at what native rank, lays the page out by turns in the order the agent listed the sources, and reports a failed Europe PMC as partial and a failed PubMed as failed, through the CLI and MCP.
