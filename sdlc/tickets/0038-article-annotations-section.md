# 0038 — Add article sections with annotations from PubTator3

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get article <input>... annotations` adds a `Section` of up to 20 normalized genes, variants, diseases and chemicals from PubTator3's export, each with its identifier and mention count, and lands the article section grammar (`all`, `--section`, `more` and one section step on each card) that 0040, 0043 and 0044 extend, through the CLI and MCP.

- PM decision: 2026-10-24. Article sections follow 0026's grammar: trailing lowercase words `annotations` and `all`, `--section` repeating, the first positional word always an input, `all` expanding to every landed section in enum order. A card's `more` lists the unrequested sections that can have data, and its `next` holds one `get article <PMID> <section…>` step naming them. A card with no PMID gets `NotApplicable`. `Section<Annotations>` holds `Capped<AnnotatedEntity>` with at most 20 items and the total; each entity has `kind` (gene, variant, disease, chemical), `name`, `count` of mentions and, as PubTator3 gives them, `entrez_id` for a gene or a variant's gene, `mesh` for a disease or chemical, and `hgvs` and `rsid` for a variant. Entities are grouped by kind and identifier, ordered by count, then kind in that order, then name. Species and other types are dropped without a problem. A variant with neither HGVS nor rsID is dropped with a problem. A PMID with no annotations or missing from the reply is `Empty` with no record. One export call carries every resolved PMID of the command, at most 20.
