## Outcome

`search drug trastuzumab --region who` and `get drug NAME regulatory --region who` return WHO prequalified finished pharmaceutical product rows from a synced WHO bundle, as 0.9 did, each with its native WHO reference number, product name, active ingredients, strength, dosage form, applicant, prequalification date and therapeutic area as sent. `biomcp sync who` downloads WHO's finished pharmaceutical products CSV, validates its header set and replaces the local copy atomically with a sync receipt. A read never downloads.

- Why: an outside user asked for WHO prequalification in public issue 235, and 0.9 shipped it as `--region who` and fixed its sync in issue 288. The drug slice dropped it only because no question in the bank asked about it. 0.9 is the quality floor, so a shipped answer to a real user request comes back.
- Starts from: drug.md's WHO prequalified medicines row and its research capture (trastuzumab BT-ON001); 0.9's WHO PQ source, header handling and region behavior; the EMA bundle (0131) as the pattern for a regional bundle; issues 235 and 288.
- Terms first: before any adapter code, the developer reads WHO's published terms for the prequalification list exports and records the exact grant in drug.md. If no grant is published, the source registers as `restricted` with WHO's copyright policy linked, and the PM is told. No commercial grant is inferred.
- Rules: WHO rows are source-scoped prequalification records, never US or EU authorizations and never merged into the FDA or EMA card. Matching is by the native product name or active ingredient field, case-insensitive and whole-field or whole-word, with no synonym expansion. `--region all` returns each region's rows separately with its own source outcome. 0.9's `who sync` spelling names `biomcp sync who`. A missing or stale bundle reports its own outcome and names the sync. The API and vaccine exports stay out of scope.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. One bounded keyless pm job downloads the export once with zero retries and archives it to Yellow outside Git. Cases assert trastuzumab's row, a whole-field miss as a verified no-match, `--region all` with separate outcomes, a missing bundle naming the sync, a header change failing the sync and keeping the prior copy, and `who sync` naming the new spelling. CLI and MCP outputs are measured with ceilings at measured +15 percent. Help and the drug ledger record the restored region. No licensed content enters the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
- PM ruling 2026-10-25 on scope: follow the drug slice's settled pattern of explicit source selection, not regions. Register one new source atom `who-pq` and one bundle `biomcp sync who-pq`. `search drug TEXT --source who-pq` returns a page of native WHO rows, matching TEXT case-insensitively as whole words against the native product name and the native combined INN, dosage form and strength cell, kept whole as sent, with no synonym, alias or brand resolution. `get drug REFERENCE --source who-pq` takes the exact native WHO reference number (for example BT-ON001) and returns that product's card with its native rows. No DrugRegion type, no `--region all` fan-out, no merged card and no regional union request. The existing `--region` retirement gains `--region who`, which names `--source who-pq`; `--region us/eu/all` keep their current retirement. 0.9's `who sync` names `biomcp sync who-pq`. Existing `--source` contracts stay unchanged. Terms are `restricted` with WHO's copyright policy linked, as the terms check found no export grant. The outcome text above that says `--region who` is replaced by this ruling.
