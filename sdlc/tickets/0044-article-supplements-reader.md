# 0044 — Add the supplements section and the article supplement reader

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get article <input> supplements` lists each supplementary file the PMC OA bucket holds with its name, kind, label, caption and the article's license, and `article supplement <ID> <FILE>` returns an xlsx, csv, tsv or txt file's sheets with row counts, a page of rows with `--rows A-B`, or the rows matching `--find TEXT` with sheet, row number, header row and the file's SHA-256, matching a protein change in its one-letter, three-letter and `p.` forms, through the CLI and MCP.
