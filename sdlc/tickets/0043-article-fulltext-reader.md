# 0043 — Add the fulltext outline and the article text reader from the PMC OA bucket

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get article <input> fulltext` returns the license and an outline of the body's parts, tables and figure captions from the PMC OA bucket, and `article text <ID> --part N` and `article text <ID> --find TEXT` return one part, split at 4,000 tokens with a continuation step, or the matching paragraphs and table rows with their part numbers, telling a paper with no PMCID (`not_applicable`) from one the bucket does not hold (`unavailable`, `not_open`), through the CLI and MCP.
