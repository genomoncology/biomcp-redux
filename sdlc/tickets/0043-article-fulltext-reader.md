# 0043 — Add the fulltext outline and the article text reader from the PMC OA bucket

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get article <input> fulltext` returns the license and an outline of the body's parts, tables and figure captions from the PMC OA bucket, and `article text <ID> --part N` and `article text <ID> --find TEXT` return one part as Markdown, split at 4,000 tokens with a continuation step, or the matching paragraphs and tables as Markdown with their part numbers, with headings, lists, emphasis, superscripts, subscripts, captions and citation markers kept by one JATS-to-Markdown converter that also serves the card's abstract, telling a paper with no PMCID (`not_applicable`) from one the bucket does not hold (`unavailable`, `not_open`), through the CLI and MCP.

A JATS reader in `jats/` turns the body into parts: each top-level `sec` in order, each `table-wrap` its own part after the section that cites it first, and any part over 4,000 tokens split at paragraph boundaries into numbered continuations. `Section<FullText>` holds `license`, `parts` (number, title, kind, tokens) and `figures` (label and caption). `article text` takes a PMID or PMCID (a PMID costs one ESummary for its PMCID), `--part N` or `--find TEXT`, never both; `--part` returns that part's text with `license` and a step to the continuation when split; `--find` matches without case and returns up to 25 hits, each a paragraph or table row with its part number, and a page step. The card's section step and `more` name `fulltext` only when the card has a PMCID, and a `fulltext` section's `next` holds `article text <PMCID> --part 1`.

- PM decision, 2026-10-24, the Markdown ruling (article.md section 5, "Body text as Markdown"): the JATS reader converts each part to Markdown. A text part is `{ part, heading_path, markdown }`. Section titles become headings at their depth, `list` becomes Markdown list items, `bold` and `italic` become `**` and `*`, `sup` and `sub` stay as `<sup>` and `<sub>`, a citation `xref` prints its marker in square brackets and a `sup` holding only citation markers prints the brackets alone, and figure captions print as `**Figure N.** caption`. A body table appears once, as its label and caption followed by a Markdown table, with a spanning cell repeated across its columns; body tables carry no separate row arrays. A `--find` hit in a paragraph quotes the paragraph's Markdown; a hit inside a table quotes the caption, the header row and the matching rows as a small Markdown table with the part number. biodata gains the `Markdown` text newtype, and the Markdown view prints a `Markdown` field unescaped under `--format md`.
