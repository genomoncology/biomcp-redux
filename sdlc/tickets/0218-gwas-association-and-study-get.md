# 0218 — Get a GWAS association with loci and a study with associations and ancestries

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 4

Depends on: 0205
Depends on: 0230
Depends on: 0234

## Outcome

`get gwas association:6114` returns the exact association card from the existing adapter: association ID, study accession, every effect allele and trait, the reported effect and interval, the exact p value, and the study and publication IDs. `get gwas association:6110 loci` returns the source locus descriptions, strongest-risk-allele strings and author-reported genes with their stable IDs, such as FGFR2 with ENSG00000066468 and Entrez 2263. `get gwas GCST000035` returns the exact study card: accession, PMID, reported and mapped traits, discovery and replication sample text, ancestry summaries, genotyping technologies, SNP count, flags and summary-statistics availability. `get gwas GCST000035 associations` returns one native association page of three for that study in p-value-ascending order, with a continuation that keeps the checked study and source query. `get gwas GCST000035 ancestries` reads the one unpaged source ancestry collection once and returns a local page of its rows, with `--section-limit` and `--section-offset` paging the retained collection with no extra provider request.

- Why: a search row names one source assertion, and the GWAS rulings accept `get gwas` for an association or a study with the `loci`, `associations` and `ancestries` sections, so a reader can confirm allele, effect, study and population context.
- Starts from: gwas.md's command tree, fields proved by capture, identity and joins and measured disclosure costs, with the GWAS rulings in `decisions/2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md`; the j14220, j14229 and j14249 captures and 0205's study collection witness; 0234's dispatch registration and adapter; 0230's types; next steps in `architecture.md` 6.10.
- Rules: every get compares the returned association ID or study accession with the requested one, and drops a mismatch with a problem and no cache admission. Every ancestry row's self link must belong to the requested study, or the row is dropped with a problem. An ancestry collection over the admission bound returns an incomplete problem, never a silently cut page, and the source receives no invented size parameter. The default study card keeps only the source ancestry summaries, and recruitment detail sits behind `ancestries`. Native sample text stays unchanged and is never corrected, summed or deduplicated, so GCST000035's repeated control label and GCST000113's differing replication counts stay visible. Source effect labels stay unchanged, so 6063's per-copy label beside the paper's heterozygote contrast is never reconciled. Author-reported and mapped genes stay separate, and neither is a causal-gene assertion. A study is fetched only by its explicit get or section, and a publication title is not promised. Next steps follow architecture 6.10: an association card offers its study card, exact-rsID variant gets and the publication, and never its own get; `loci` offers exact gene pivots only from returned stable IDs; a study card offers `associations` and `ancestries`.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Before get code, one bounded keyless pm job captures each case's exact request once, with zero retries, and records the replies. The 6114 case asserts rs3817198-C, GCST000035 and PMID 17529967; Q18 to Q20 assert 6063's rs6983267-G and its source-labeled effect and interval; Q21 asserts 11386's rs1333049-C with OR 1.36 and p=3×10⁻¹⁹; Q06 to Q08 assert 6110's locus row with FGFR2's stable IDs and the GCST000035 study card with its native sample text; Q11 asserts GCST000037's replication ancestry categories; Q16 and Q17 assert GCST000113's native sample text; Q22 asserts GCST000057's single native trait label; a GCST000035 ancestries case asserts three source rows and a local walk with no gap or duplicate; a GCST000035 associations case asserts the study page and its continuation. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases assert a failed outcome with source and terms. The job measures each command in CLI and MCP output, starting from the projection ceilings of 1,248 bytes for the 6114 card, 1,666 for the GCST000035 card, 2,875 for its ancestry section and 735 for the 6110 locus section, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in gwas.md. Real replies replay from outside the repo through `--cases DIR`, and synthetic replies prove failure mechanics. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
