# 0222 — Sync the WikiPathways metadata catalogs and search and get WikiPathways records

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 4

Depends on: 0209
Depends on: 0212
Depends on: 0216

## Outcome

`biomcp sync wikipathways` downloads the two captured WikiPathways metadata catalogs, the search catalog and the get catalog, validates each, keeps each with its own hash and revision, and replaces the local pair atomically with a sync receipt that reports hash, bytes, revision and human and total row counts and no catalog body. `search pathway "Apoptosis" --source wikipathways` returns a five-row page of human catalog headers from the stored search catalog, filtered by the exact source species field, in catalog order, paged with `--limit`, `--offset` and `--pool` with the bundle revision kept. `get pathway WP382` returns the checked WP identity and native human metadata from the stored get catalog, and `get pathway WP382 summary` returns the catalog description labeled as source-truncated. Repeated `--source reactome --source wikipathways` returns both legs through the established multi-source round-robin order with no score blending and no cross-source merge.

- Why: the 2026-10-26 pathway rulings add `sync wikipathways` for the two metadata catalogs, and the design keeps WikiPathways as a distinct source that is requested explicitly.
- Starts from: pathway.md's bundles section, WikiPathways rows of the source measurements and harvest table, public fields and case matrix; the 2026-10-26 pathway rulings; the j14201 catalog captures of 2,218 rows with 1,131 human rows; the shared `sync <bundle>` contract and stored-only bundle read API in `architecture.md` 4.11; 0172 and 0173 as the pattern; 0216's dispatch registration; 0209's types and 0212's registry.
- Rules: a requested WP ID must match a stored catalog row exactly, and a non-human row is never a card. The search matches the unchanged phrase against source titles only, with no translation of a Reactome name and no synonym. Catalog `datanodes` and annotation strings are not checked gene IDs and never populate a genes section. A copied ellipsis in a description is labeled source truncation and never presented as the whole explanation. The catalogs are published snapshots, never proof of every curated or historical pathway. The xref catalog is not used. Reads never trigger a sync and make no network request; a failed validation keeps the old usable copy; source disabling applies before any local read. An absent, stale or syncing bundle answers with its state and a `sync` step. Records from the two sources never merge, even when their titles match. `genes` on a WP ID fails before I/O until 0224 lands, and `events` on a WP ID always fails before I/O.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Before adapter code, one bounded keyless pm job syncs the bundle once and records the receipt. A receipt case asserts the two catalog hashes, bytes and human and total row counts on the capture date; the harvest queries assert their WikiPathways literal-title match counts, including `Apoptosis`, `Autophagy` and `Metabolism`, and a zero for `Signaling by EGFR`; the sentinel `zzzzNoPathway0191` asserts a zero; `get pathway WP382` asserts the human identity and the summary asserts the truncation label; an unknown WP ID asserts not found; a non-human catalog row asserts refusal; a missing bundle names `biomcp sync wikipathways`; a union case asserts both legs in round-robin order and a failed leg named beside a successful one. Sync cases cover a synthetic partial download, a malformed catalog, a failed second catalog and two concurrent readers during one replacement, with zero requests on reads. The job measures each command in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design allocations of 700 tokens for a header page, 450 for the card, 650 for the summary and 300 for the sync receipt, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in pathway.md; a measurement over its allocation goes to the PM before landing. WikiPathways content uses CC0, and the full catalogs replay from outside the repo through `--cases DIR` because of their size, while synthetic files prove sync mechanics. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
