# 0051 — Add article citation-evidence and article recommendations

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`article citation-evidence <CITING> <CITED>` returns every context where one paper cites another, with intents and the influential flag from Semantic Scholar, and with `--fulltext` also the citing paper's own paragraphs that cite the reference, as Markdown from the PMC OA bucket; `article recommendations <ID>... [--negative ID]... [--limit N]` returns the papers Semantic Scholar recommends in its own order; each tells an empty answer from a source failure, with or without the user's key, through the CLI and MCP.

- PM decision: 2026-10-24. `ArticleEvidence` holds `citing`, `cited` and `fulltext` (boolean). The query reads the citing paper's Semantic Scholar references (pages of 1,000, at most five calls), keeps the row whose PubMed ID equals the cited PMID or whose DOI equals the cited paper's ESummary DOI without ASCII case, and returns `contexts` whole, `intents` and `influential`. No matching row is complete and empty with notice `not_in_references`; a matching row with no contexts is complete and empty with notice `no_contexts`. With `--fulltext`, a second part reads the citing paper's JATS through 0043, finds the `ref` whose `pub-id` of type `pmid` or `doi` equals the cited identifier, and returns every paragraph with an `xref` to that `ref`, as Markdown with its part number; a citing paper with no PMCID gives that part `NotApplicable`, and one outside the bucket `Unavailable` with reason `not_open`, beside Semantic Scholar's part. `ArticleRecommend` holds `seeds` (1 to 10), `negative` (0 to 10), `limit` (1 to 100, default 10), `offset` and `pool`; one seed and no negative asks `forpaper`, anything else asks the POST, both with `fields=externalIds,title,year`; the pool keeps Semantic Scholar's order with ESummary headers or Semantic Scholar's own with `types_unknown`; the query echo states that Semantic Scholar recommends from its pool of recent papers; zero results is complete with total 0 and notice `no_recommendations`. A 429 past the retries fails either command with reason `rate_limited` and a `key` step; a 404 seed is `not_found` for that input. A card's `next` adds `article recommendations <PMID>`, and a Semantic Scholar edge item whose `contexts.total` exceeds one adds `article citation-evidence <CITING> <CITED>`.
