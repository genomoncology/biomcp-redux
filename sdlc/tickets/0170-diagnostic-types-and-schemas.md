## Outcome

`biodata` holds the neutral, no-I/O diagnostic records that `planning/slices/diagnostic.md` names, as a tagged union of record kinds: `GtrTestVersion` keyed by the complete GTR accession plus version, `GtrTestRelation` for native gene, condition and method rows, `WhoIvdProduct` keyed by WHO Product ID with every native product code row, `DeviceSubmission` for an FDA PMA record keyed by `pma_number` plus `supplement_number`, and `CompanionDiagnosticRelation` for one physical FDA companion table row. A GTR condition's `GTR_identifier` maps to the shared `Concept` with a `Curie` such as `MedGen:C2676676` and its source label; a gene row's numeric `gene_or_SNOMED_CT_ID` maps to the shared `GeneRef` with `NcbiGeneId`, labeled by `gene_symbol`; the same column on a condition row is SNOMED CT and never a gene ID. `biomcp-types` holds the consumer side: the `search diagnostic` request with a required `--source gtr|fda-cdx`, the GTR selectors `--gene-id` (exclusive with `--gene`), `--gene`, `--condition-id` accepting only a MedGen CUI, and `--type`, the `fda-cdx` selectors `--device-id P150013[/S020]`, `--drug-application` and `--biomarker`, and `--limit` and `--offset` with a default of five; the `get diagnostic` request with an ID, a required `--source gtr|who-ivd|fda-pma`, the GTR sections `genes`, `conditions` and `methods`, the PMA `--supplement` selector and sections `regulatory` and `supplements`, and `--section-limit` and `--section-offset`; the GTR header and conflict row, the GTR card, the WHO card, the PMA base and supplement cards, the companion association page and each section page. The generated, checked-in schemas are `biomcp.diagnostic_page/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_card/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_genes/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_conditions/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_methods/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_regulatory/1`, `biomcp.diagnostic_supplements/1` and `biomcp.diagnostic_companion/1`. Sync reuses `biomcp.sync/2`, and the GTR receipt's duplicate identity count follows the compatibility check.

- Why: the slice method lands types and schema before adapters, and the accepted design with the 0154 PM review and recheck amendments fixes the records, the selectors and the page shapes.
- Starts from: diagnostic.md's identity and source ownership table, reviewed types, field lists, JSON shapes and selector table, with the 0154 amendments, which win over the design text; the j12632 capture and its column optionality inventories on the evidence store, which hold each kept source's exact-request captures; `rust-standards.md`; the shared `Concept`, `Curie`, `GeneRef` and `NcbiGeneId`.
- Rules: companion and GTR relations are records, never `Statement` values. A label alone never acquires a `Curie` or a gene ID. GTR current-version, test-current, test-public, laboratory-current and laboratory-public flags stay separate native fields, and a CLIA number never reads as authorization. An identity conflict is its own status with no card. A PMA base row keeps its empty `supplement_number` distinct from an absent one, and no `S000` is made up. A companion row keeps every whole cell keyed by its own table's exact headers, the two tables stay distinct kinds, and `device_ids` and `drug_applications` exist only where the row's own cells and links establish them. A drug application keeps its published `NDA` or `BLA` prefix and six digits with any leading zero, and its type is never inferred from the number or a drug name. K and DEN numbers are source facts with no get. `fda-510k` stays reserved and is not advertised. `--drug-cell` does not exist. `--biomarker` exists only if 0174 asserts its captured positive; otherwise it is deferred. `--supplement` with the `supplements` section, a GTR selector with `fda-cdx`, an `fda-cdx` selector with `gtr`, a section with `who-ivd`, a non-MedGen `--condition-id` and `--gene` with `--gene-id` fail before I/O. There is no `all` source or section and no `--full`.
