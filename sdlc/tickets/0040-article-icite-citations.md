# 0040 — Sort by citations and follow the citation graph with iCite

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`search article … --sort citations` orders the whole pool by iCite's count of citing PubMed papers and refuses above 10,000 PMIDs before any iCite request, `get article <input> citations` adds iCite's counts as a section, and `article citations <PMID>` and `article references <PMID>` page the citing and cited papers most cited first with a total, through the CLI and MCP.
