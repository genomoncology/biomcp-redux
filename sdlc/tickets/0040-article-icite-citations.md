- PM decision: 2026-10-24. `Source::Icite` (`icite`) lands here. `sort` widens to `citations` for every source. iCite runs in batches of 200 with `fl` limited to the fields above, four batches at a time, over every PMID the pool holds, and stores counts with the pool items; ties keep the `rank` order, and items with no PMID or missing from iCite come last with no `cited_by`. Above 10,000 PMIDs the search is a usage error, code `usage.too_many_pmids`, that names the count and asks to narrow, with no iCite request. `ArticleItem` gains `cited_by`, present only under `citations`. A default page's `next` adds the same search with `--sort citations` after `get article`; the step carries no prose, and `--help` for `--sort` states the cost of about 1 s per 1,000 PMIDs. `Section<Citations>` holds `cited_by`, `cited_by_clinical`, `references` (the count) and `rcr` when not provisional. `article citations` and `article references` are one `ArticleEdges` query with `pmid`, `limit`, `offset` and `pool`, build a pool from iCite's lists ordered most cited first, take headers from ESummary per page and report the total. A card's `next` adds `article citations <PMID>`.
