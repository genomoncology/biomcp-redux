# 0027 — Add the protein section from UniProt by HGNC accessions

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`get gene <input> protein` returns every reviewed UniProt protein that HGNC lists for the gene, with accession, name, length, function text and subcellular location, and `not_applicable` with no request when HGNC lists none, proven by gene.md cases 11 and 12 under their ceilings.
