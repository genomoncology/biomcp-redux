Status: COMPLETE.

## Outcome

`search article … --source …,litsense` folds LitSense2's sentence-similarity order into the pool, each LitSense2 item carrying the sentence that matched and its section, and `search article … --source …,pubtator` folds PubTator3's search order, sending genes as the Entrez ID HGNC resolution gives and the words as typed; help describes each source's job and its known failure modes without ranking sources, neither source is ever part of the default, and each refuses before any request a filter it cannot apply, through the CLI and MCP.

- PM decision: 2026-10-24. `Source::LitSense` (`litsense`) lands here, and `source` widens to `litsense` and `pubtator`. LitSense2 sends the words, `--gene` as typed, `--variant`, `--disease` and `--drug` as one query with `rerank=true`, reads its one reply of 100 sentences, keeps each paper at the rank of its best sentence, and sets `ArticleItem.sentence` to `{ section, text }` from that sentence. Help for `--source` gains the two lines from article.md section 5, which describe each job and failure mode and rank nothing. A LitSense2 call that outlasts the leg's deadline or answers 5xx fails that leg only.
