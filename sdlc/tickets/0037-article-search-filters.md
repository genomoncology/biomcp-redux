# 0037 — Add PubMed filters and date order to search article

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`search article` takes `--author`, `--journal`, `--since`, `--until`, `--type` and `--sort date`, sends each to PubMed as its own field tag or sort inside the echoed term, and returns PubMed's own order for that term, through the CLI and MCP.

- Checked boundaries, 2026-10-24, ESearch with `tool=biomcp`: `AKT1 AND Carpten[au]` by relevance returned 22505016, 17611497, 18053070. `AKT1 E17K AND Nature[ta]` returned 39506119 and 17611497, and PubMed maps `Nature[ta]` to three journals in `translationset`. `KRAS G12C sotorasib AND 2024:3000[dp]` returned 331, and with no `sort` the first five were 42891951, 42890422, 42870846, 42860233 and 42814800, the most recently added. `AKT1 E17K AND 1800:2010[dp]` returned 36 including 17611497. `next-generation sequencing advanced cancer ESMO AND (guideline[pt] OR "practice guideline"[pt])` returned only 38834388. `AKT1 E17K AND "clinical trial"[pt]` returned 7, led by 33377972 and 35440569.
- PM decision: 2026-10-24. Each filter joins the term with ` AND ` after the words: `--author X` as `X[au]`, `--journal X` as `X[ta]`, `--since` and `--until` as one `A:B[dp]` range whose open end is 1800 or 3000, `--type` as `[pt]` tags: `trial` → `"clinical trial"[pt]`, `randomized` → `"randomized controlled trial"[pt]`, `review` → `review[pt]`, `systematic_review` → `"systematic review"[pt]`, `meta_analysis` → `"meta-analysis"[pt]`, `guideline` → `(guideline[pt] OR "practice guideline"[pt])`, `case_report` → `"case reports"[pt]`. Dates take `PartialDate` text and print as PubMed's `YYYY/MM/DD` to the stated precision. `sort` widens to `rank` and `date`; `date` sends ESearch no `sort`, which PubMed orders by most recently added, and the term echo stays the same. A filter alone is a valid search.
- Keeps: 0035's term for searches without these options, and every 0035 and 0036 case.
- Proof: Record live once into `tests/replies/article/filters/`; replies hold PMIDs and citation metadata, no abstracts. `search article AKT1 --author Carpten` asserts the term `AKT1 AND Carpten[au]` and 17611497 within the first 3 (case 29). `search article "AKT1 E17K" --journal Nature` asserts 17611497 within the first 2. `search article "KRAS G12C sotorasib" --since 2024 --sort date --limit 5` asserts five items each dated 2024 or later, `query.sort` date, the recorded ESearch URL without `sort`, and a page step (case 26), and runs through MCP with parity. `search article "AKT1 E17K" --until 2010` asserts total 36 and 17611497 included. `search article "next-generation sequencing advanced cancer ESMO" --type guideline` asserts 38834388 first and every item typed practice_guideline or guideline (case 27). `search article "AKT1 E17K" --type trial` asserts every item typed with a clinical trial type. A `--since` later than `--until` is a usage error with no request. Ceilings 7,000 bytes per page, 3,500 for the page of five. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job after current main.
- Defers: Each filter's mapping for other sources, and `unsupported_filter`, go to the ticket that adds each source (0039, 0041, 0042). `--sort citations` goes to 0040.

- Reviewed dates: Compare since/until inclusively at stated PartialDate precision before any request, and prove a filter-only search. Preserve the exact field-tag term and common parser/speller. The [PubMed guide](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/help/) distinguishes most-recently-added from publication date; date sort keeps the approved provider order. Publication-type tags include narrower types, so phase-specific trial types are valid. A displayed print date alone does not prove a dp-filter violation because eligible electronic dates also participate.
