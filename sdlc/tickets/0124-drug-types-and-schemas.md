# 0124 — Add the drug types and generated schemas

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 3

## Outcome

`biodata` holds the neutral drug records that `planning/slices/drug.md` section 4 names: `DrugIdentity`, `DrugIdentityRelation`, `DrugProduct`, `DrugLabelReference`, `DrugLabelSection`, `DrugTargetRelation`, `DrugIndicationClaim`, `DrugInteractionClaim` and a source-scoped `AuthorizationEvent` that keeps its native regulator record, date roles, jurisdiction and original status. A closed `identity_level` set names the design's identity levels plus unknown. `biomcp-types` holds the consumer side: the `search drug` request with `--source`, `--limit` and `--offset`, the `get drug` request with a required `--source` and the named sections, the candidate header, the exact-ID card, the section pages, and the generated, checked-in schemas `biomcp.drug_page/1`, `biomcp.drug_card/1`, `biomcp.drug_label/1`, `biomcp.drug_targets/1`, `biomcp.drug_indications/1`, `biomcp.drug_civic/1`, `biomcp.drug_approvals/1`, `biomcp.drug_regulatory/1` and `biomcp.drug_interactions/1`. Sync reuses `biomcp.sync/2`.

- Why: the slice method lands types and schema before adapters, and the accepted drug design fixes the records, the header and card field lists and the section names.
- Starts from: drug.md sections 2 and 4 with the 0116 PM recheck amendments; `rust-standards.md`; the existing variant and gene records in `biodata` and their consumer types in `biomcp-types`.
- Rules: the header carries only `id` in published form, `source`, source-native `name`, `identity_level`, optional native `type` and optional record URL. The card adds only source-native alternate names, cross-references with relation and provenance, parent, product and formulation links the source states, record version and retrieval metadata, and available sections and next steps. Neither carries an authorization event, an approval flag or date, label text, interaction rows or a merged target list. `AuthorizationEvent` appears only in the `approvals` and `regulatory` section types. Identifiers keep their published forms, such as `CHEMBL941`, `EMEA/H/C/000406`, `NDA021335`, an SPL set UUID and `DDInter1744`, with the source in a separate field and no `SOURCE:ID` grammar. Label and interaction continuation reuse the shipped `section_offset` and `section_limit` spelling. `identity_level` comes from the endpoint or record type, never from a field's shape; an unknown level stays unknown. The section set has no `all`, `shortage`, `safety` or `cell_lines`.
- Proof: round-trip tests for each identifier grammar and each closed set, including refusal of a prefixed ID; a test that the header and card types have no authorization, label text or interaction field; the generated schemas checked in with snapshot and compatibility checks; existing schemas unchanged. No adapter or provider call. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
