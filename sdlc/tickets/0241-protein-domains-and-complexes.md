# 0241 — Add InterPro domains, Complex Portal complexes and complex members

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 4

Depends on: 0235
Depends on: 0245

## Outcome

`protein domains P15056` sends InterPro's accession-scoped entry request and returns five InterPro matches per page with each IPR accession, native name and type, source protein accession, length and taxon, and every native location group and fragment. `--type domain` selects InterPro's native type, natively if 0229 proved the type selection and otherwise over the complete retained collection. Paging follows the source's next pointer as 0229 proved it. `protein complexes P38398` sends Complex Portal's human accession search and returns five admitted complex headers per page, each with its CPX accession, source name, organism, native predicted flag and actual participant total. A complex is admitted only when its native participants include the exact query protein. `protein complex-members P38398 --complex CPX-715` returns five native members per page of that one complex, with participant identifier, type, taxon, name, native stoichiometry text and supplied cross-references, reading the retained search reply or one Complex Portal detail request when the search reply lacks the participants. `get protein P15056 domains` and `complexes` return the same first page under `--section-limit`, and the card from 0245 gains its steps for both sections.

- Why: P06, P16 and P21 ask which source domains and families InterPro reports, and P08, P09, P12, P18 and P28 ask which complexes the source reports a protein in. The 2026-10-26 protein rulings keep InterPro location matches and Complex Portal exact-member access with `complex-members` under an anchor that must be an actual participant.
- Starts from: protein.md's command tree, case table and measured budget section, with the 2026-10-26 protein rulings; the j14202 InterPro and Complex Portal captures, the j14211 and j14221 CPX-715 detail capture and 0229's paging, type and absence witnesses; 0235's registry; 0245's dispatch registration; 0233's types.
- Rules: an InterPro family, site, repeat or superfamily never becomes a domain, fragments are never flattened, and no first overlapping fragment is chosen. The native count and the filtered count stay separate. A domain-type selection with no rows is a successful empty and never a missing source. Search text and description never admit a complex. The predicted flag stays literal, and predicted complexes are never called curated. An admitted total is complete only after the whole query collection is scanned, and a search total is never an admitted total. A complex search reply with no hits is a source-local empty that says nothing about every biological complex. The `complex-members` anchor must be an actual participant of the named complex; otherwise the command returns a problem naming the anchor and the complex and no member page. A detail reply whose complex identity differs from the request is dropped with a problem and never cached. Non-protein members stay typed source facts with no protein step, and a member offers a protein pivot only for a supported exact protein ID that round-trips. No 93-member default: members always page. A complexes page gives one member step per displayed complex, then the page step. A disabled InterPro or Complex Portal source makes its section skipped, never empty, and its card step is not offered.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Cases reuse the j14202, j14211, j14221 and 0229 captures where the request matches exactly, and one bounded keyless pm job captures once, with zero retries, any exact request a case needs that those archives lack. P06 asserts P15056's eleven InterPro entries across their native types; P16 asserts P04637 IPR002117 as family and IPR010991 as domain with `--type domain` selecting only the latter; P21 asserts Q8N726's single family IPR010868 and a successful empty domain-type selection; a discontinuous fragment case asserts every location group intact; a paging case walks the 0229 InterPro pages with no gap or duplicate. P08 asserts CPX-26353 is excluded from P15056's complexes although its description mentions BRAF; P09 asserts eighteen native hits and twelve exact admissions with each predicted flag kept; P28 asserts P38398's six admissions among nine hits with CPX-715, CPX-845 and CPX-955 not predicted and three others predicted; P18 asserts P13569's HTTP 200 zero as a successful empty; P12 asserts CPX-715's BRCA1 and BARD1 participants with their exact IDs and stoichiometry text. Member cases cover a wrong anchor, a wrong complex, a mismatched detail identity, a non-protein member and pages of five over the 93-participant complex. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases for each source assert a failed outcome with source and terms. The job measures each command and section in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design ceilings of 6,659 bytes for domains, 3,577 for complexes and 3,723 for complex members, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in protein.md. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
