# 0139 — Register the disease sources and components with use labels

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 3

Depends on: 0138

## Outcome

The one source registry declares each kept disease source and each carried component once, with the use label, disable group, terms name and publisher URL from `planning/slices/disease.md`'s source and component use policy: the `ols`, `mydisease` and `monarch` services labeled `attribution`; `seer` labeled `open`; and the components `mondo` and `ncit` labeled `attribution`, `doid` labeled `open`, `hpo` and `orphanet` labeled `attribution`, and `omim` labeled `restricted`. Every source and component starts enabled. `--disable-sources`, the configured disable list and the `noncommercial` and `restricted` groups skip a source or component before any request, cache read or replay, and source outcomes and health carry the use label and terms on empty, skipped and failed outcomes too.

- Why: the open-by-default decision gives every source and component a use label and group in one registry, and the 0117 review asked for each disease source's and component's label before build.
- Starts from: `crates/biomcp-adapters/src/registry.rs`; decision `2026-10-24-sources-are-open-by-default.md`; disease.md's use policy table and the final HPO terms finding; 0125's drug registry entries as the pattern; 0138's types.
- Rules: HPO is labeled `attribution` from the publisher's current license page, which allows commercial use and requires citation, version display, preserved content and relationships, and product acknowledgement; the `restricted` group does not disable HPO annotations. `omim` applies only to a separately licensed OMIM payload, and an OMIM or Orphanet identifier in an HPO-published annotation or a MONDO cross-reference does not make that payload. Naming a provider disables the whole provider, and naming a component disables that component. Disabling an OLS ontology component removes that ontology before the request, and the MONDO default reports skipped rather than choosing another ontology. Disabling a MyDisease component removes its field projection before the request. Because no verified Monarch filter excludes a component, disabling `hpo` or `orphanet` makes a Monarch section skip before I/O, never fetch and discard. Dropped routes such as direct HPO, OncoTree, CIViC disease context, Reactome, MedlinePlus, UMLS, DisGeNET and NIH RePORTER get no new runtime declaration. Open Targets disease associations are deferred, and any existing `opentargets` declaration stays as it is. No second terms table.
- Proof: cases first. A case per declaration asserts its label, group and terms. D08-disable-ols, D13-disable-monarch, D13-disable-hpo, D13-disable-omim, D13-disable-orphanet, D13-disable-restricted, D02-disable-mondo, D02-disable-doid, D02-disable-ncit and D19-disable-seer assert the skip with zero requests and no cache or replay read, and that allowed independent sources stay available; D13-disable-restricted asserts that Monarch genes and phenotypes stay available. Stub-backed, with no provider call. This ticket adds no filter or exact-ID get; each filter and exact-ID get that later disease tickets add has a known-positive capture through one bounded keyless pm job. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
