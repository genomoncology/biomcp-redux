# 0035 — Answer search article with plain PubMed from a stored pool

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`biomcp search article [TEXT] [--gene INPUT] [--variant TEXT] [--disease TEXT] [--drug TEXT]` returns PubMed's own relevance order for the exact term it echoes, from a pool built once over BioMCP's requested 9,999-item PubMed window, with stable pages that a warm read serves without a request, a total, a truncation flag and a notice naming words PubMed did not find, through the CLI and the one MCP tool.
