# 0036 — Answer get article by PMID, PMCID and DOI

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 1

## Outcome

`biomcp get article <PMID | PMCID | DOI>...` returns one card per input, in input order, from one PubMed EFetch call, with every clinical publication type, corrections with their notice PMIDs, ten authors with the total, the whole abstract with its labels and the time the record was fetched, answers not found for an unknown PMID or a DOI whose record disagrees, and a search page's `next` gains `get article` for its first item.
