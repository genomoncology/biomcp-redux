## Consumed source details

The narrow clinical/source review confirmed the HGNC locus vocabulary at https://hgnc.genenames.org/help/symbol-report/index.html and `readthrough` at https://rest.genenames.org/fetch/symbol/ZNF511-PRAP1 . Canonical enum spellings live in gene/values.rs; source spellings map in the future adapter. The reviewed HGNC values are `gene with protein product`, `RNA, cluster`, `RNA, long non-coding`, `RNA, micro`, `RNA, misc`, `RNA, ribosomal`, `RNA, small nuclear`, `RNA, small nucleolar`, `RNA, transfer`, `RNA, vault`, `RNA, Y`, `phenotype only`, `pseudogene`, `complex locus constituent`, `endogenous retrovirus`, `fragile site`, `immunoglobulin gene`, `immunoglobulin pseudogene`, `readthrough`, `region`, `T cell receptor gene`, `T cell receptor pseudogene`, `transposable element`, `unknown`, and `virus integration site`. The source-stated `unknown` is distinct from missing or unrecognized values. Required identity fields remain required; an unfamiliar required identity needs a later adapter problem/refusal rather than a fabricated value.

MyGene assigns genomic_pos to GRCh38 and genomic_pos_hg19 to GRCh37: https://mygene.info/blog/mygene-info-moved-to-grch38hg38-for-human-genes-with-hg19-still-supported . Ensembl defines forward/reverse strands and permits chromosome or scaffold names: https://jun2026.archive.ensembl.org/info/website/upload/gff.html . Contig preserves a bounded printable ASCII token without normalization or membership inference; the adapter owns mappings. NCBI MANE documents patch accessions and curated NM_/NR_ members: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/refseq/MANE/ . General transcripts retain optional versions; ManeSelect requires both versions and curated RefSeq prefixes, without asserting actual MANE membership.

Cytoband checks single nuclear band syntax, including long subdivisions in https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg38/database/cytoBand.txt.gz . Ranges and descriptors remain unrepresentable optional locations, as the DMD range demonstrates: https://rest.genenames.org/fetch/symbol/DMD . The source text must not be truncated into one endpoint by the later adapter.
