# 0220 — Add the phenotype synonyms, xrefs and hierarchy sections

Status: OPEN.

Milestone: 4

Depends on: 0217

## Outcome

`get phenotype HP:ID synonyms --source hpo` returns HPO's flat, unclassified synonym list and `--source monarch` returns Monarch's synonyms with their exact, broad, narrow and related classes. `get phenotype HP:ID xrefs --source hpo` returns HPO's cross-references, and `--source monarch` returns Monarch's cross-references with their native predicates only if 0208 proved a positive Monarch xref. `get phenotype HP:ID hierarchy --source monarch` returns the direct parent and child links, each with the related term's exact ID, label and native relation, in native order. Each section reads the same exact entity request as its source's card and pages that retained reply locally with `--section-limit` and `--section-offset`, five rows or links by default, with no extra provider request. The HPO and Monarch cards from 0217 gain their section steps.

- Why: P02, P05 and P16 need synonyms and cross-references as a separate disclosure layer, P03 and P16 need the hierarchy, and the PM rulings keep these sections with five-row defaults and parent and child links rather than whole native nodes.
- Starts from: phenotype.md's synonym, xref and hierarchy rows, source fields table and progressive disclosure section, with the Phenotype rulings in `2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md`; the j14204 and j14227 replies and 0208's xref witness; 0211's types and 0217's adapters and dispatch.
- Rules: an HPO synonym never takes a Monarch class. HPO's Epilepsy synonym for Seizure and Neurofibromatosis synonym for Neurofibroma stay source synonyms and grant no clinical equivalence, and Monarch's related class for Epilepsy stays as sent. A cross-reference proves no equivalence. A hierarchy link holds no whole native node, and no local graph traversal runs. HPO's descendant count of 346 and Monarch's of 347 for Seizure stay separate source counts and are never patient prevalence. An empty list stays distinct from an absent field. Local pages stay bound to the retained entity reply. Without 0208's positive Monarch xref, `xrefs --source monarch` keeps its usage error and the deferral is recorded.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Cases replay the retained exact card replies. A case whose exact request those jobs did not send gets one bounded keyless pm job capture with zero retries before section code. P16 asserts HPO's unclassified Epilepsy synonym beside Monarch's related class; P02 asserts the Neurofibromatosis synonym stays a synonym; P05 asserts HPO's Macrocephaly cross-references; the Monarch xref case asserts 0208's positive row and its predicate; P16 asserts Seizure's one parent and twelve children in native order with first, next and terminal local pages and exact next arguments; an explicit limit keeps its value. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases assert a failed outcome with source and terms. The job measures each section in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design ceilings of 495 comparable bytes for synonyms and 358 for xrefs. The hierarchy ceiling comes from the measured five-link output, not the 10,921-byte whole node figure. Each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in phenotype.md. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
