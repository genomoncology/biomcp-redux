## Outcome

`search protein --gene BRAF` sends UniProt's reviewed human search on the exact gene field under taxon 9606 and returns a page of protein headers in source-native order with the native count, the echoed query and scope, and a retained continuation. `search protein TEXT` passes the text unchanged to UniProt under the same default scope. `--reviewed` spells the default explicitly, and `--include-unreviewed` widens the scope only if 0229 proved its positive. `--limit`, `--pool` and `--offset` page over the retained source-bound pool, and a source cursor stays inside retained state. `get protein P00533` returns the `ProteinCard` from UniProt's exact entry record: accession, entry ID, source protein name, source gene names, taxon, reviewed status, length, entry audit and sequence version, publisher URL, the `more` list of sections and at most five section or pivot steps, with no sequence, reference list, variant list or network. `get protein P42771 Q8N726` returns one card per accession in request order. `get protein BRAF` resolves the symbol through the landed gene entity's exact HGNC UniProt cross-references, returns the card when one accession is listed, and returns candidates with one disambiguate get each when more than one is listed. An isoform accession follows 0229's entry witness.

- Why: the first protein questions ask which record to get after a gene search and which accession and sequence version qualify a residue number, and the design answers both with source-native search and an exact identity card.
- Starts from: protein.md's command tree, gene pivot rules, delight questions, case table and measured budget section, with the 2026-10-26 protein rulings; the j14202 entry captures, the j14211 and j14221 search captures and 0229's witnesses; the existing UniProt adapter and the gene slice's HGNC UniProt cross-references; the shared transport, pools and next steps in `architecture.md` 4, 6.7 and 6.10; 0233's types.
- Rules: no symbol, synonym or gene-name repair, and no rewrite of the text query. A gene with two protein products returns both as candidates and never a first-hit guess. The card shows a sequence version only where the source record states it and never adds `-1`. A returned accession or taxon that differs from the request is dropped with a problem and never cached. A well-formed accession with no record is absent, and a failed request is failed, never absent. The UniProt adapter adds search and exact entry endpoints beside the gene section's accessions endpoint, which stays unchanged. A retained pool binds the query, source settings, filters, revision and continuation, and an expired or mismatched pool returns restart, never a silent refetch. Next steps follow `architecture.md` 6.10: a search page gives one disambiguate get per candidate on the page, then the page step, then at most three other steps; the card gives at most five section or pivot steps. Every emitted accession round-trips through its command or no step is offered. No genome build, MANE status or clinical significance is inferred from a protein record.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Cases reuse the j14202, j14211, j14221 and 0229 captures where the request matches exactly, and one bounded keyless pm job captures once, with zero retries, any exact request a case needs that those archives lack. P07 asserts `--gene BRAF` returns exactly P15056 with its get step; P20 asserts `--gene CDKN2A` returns P42771 and Q8N726 as two candidates and their exact gets return lengths 156 and 132; P03 asserts the P00533 card with sequence version 2 and length 1210; P27 asserts the Q9UIF7 card with length 546; the 0229 text search asserts the five-hit page and its second page; the 0229 widened search asserts its unreviewed human record, or `--include-unreviewed` stays deferred; `get protein CDKN2A` asserts two candidates and no card. Paging cases cover the default five, a one-row page, the full captured collection, first, middle and terminal pages, no gap or duplicate, an expired and a mismatched pool, a zero and an over-maximum limit and an invalid offset. Usage cases cover text with `--gene`, absent input and `--reviewed` with `--include-unreviewed`. A synthetic wrong accession and wrong taxon assert the drop and no cache admission, and the absent accession asserts absent distinct from failed. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases assert a failed outcome with source and terms, never an empty page, as P23 requires. The job measures each command in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design ceilings of 2,182 bytes for the card and 6,500 for a five-header search page, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in protein.md. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
