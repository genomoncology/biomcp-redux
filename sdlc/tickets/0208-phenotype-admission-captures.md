## Outcome

One bounded keyless pm job captures the five witnesses that `planning/slices/phenotype.md` still requires before the phenotype types are accepted, and the design records each one as proven, empty or failed. The witnesses are: a Monarch term search restricted to `namespace=HP` and the phenotype category at limit 5 and offset 5 for P02 neurofibroma and P16 seizure, and P02's final page at offset 15, proving a native middle and terminal page; the Monarch exact entity card for `HP:0001676`, the obsolete row in P11's search, with its native deprecation fact and any replacement it supplies; Monarch exact entity cards for `HP:0000256` and `HP:0001067`, stopping at the first one whose cross-reference field is not null, as the positive Monarch xref witness; the next page at limit 1 and offset 1 of the reverse `HP:0001250` disease association page with `direct=true` and of the reverse `HP:0001250` human gene association page with `direct=true` and the human subject taxon `NCBITaxon:9606`; and the same reverse gene request with the subject taxon `NCBITaxon:10090`, as the wrong-taxon witness. Raw replies, headers, the ledger and the recipe go to the evidence store, and no product code changes.

- Why: the design names a native middle page, an exact obsolete card, a positive Monarch xref, an association next page and a wrong-taxon rejection as admission work, and the slice method requires each kept source's exact-request captures before its shared types are accepted.
- Starts from: phenotype.md's acceptance cases by command and option, its native candidate findings and its evidence and known limits, with the Phenotype section of decision `2026-10-26-protein-pathway-study-rulings.md`, which wins over the design text; the j14204, j14210 and j14227 captures and their exact request inventories on the evidence store; the Monarch declaration in `crates/biomcp-adapters/src/monarch/mod.rs`.
- Rules: the recipe names each exact URL before the job runs, sends each row once in order with one second between admissions, a 15-second deadline and a 4 MiB body bound, and makes at most nine physical requests. No retry, redirect, altered query, fallback host or extra page. No selector is sent empty, so the j14210 empty `subject_taxon=` defect cannot repeat. A 429 or a server error stops the job. No credential, cookie or bundle download. A successful search is not repeated. A failed or empty witness keeps its capability deferred: without the middle page, search paging past the first page stays unproven; without the obsolete card, the Monarch card carries no deprecation field and offers no replacement action; without a positive xref, the Monarch `xrefs` section stays deferred; without the association next page, association page steps stay unproven; without the wrong-taxon reply, the wrong-taxon rejection case uses a synthetic reply labeled synthetic. No clinical answer is inferred from a capture.
- Proof: the job ledger records every attempt with its exact URL, status, bytes, SHA-256 and timing, and the archive path and hash appear in phenotype.md beside each witness's result, and through pm progress. A reviewer checks that each witness's result names the capability it unlocks or defers. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
