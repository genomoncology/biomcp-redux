## Outcome

One bounded keyless pm job captures the witnesses that `planning/slices/study.md` and the 2026-10-26 study rulings require before the gene-count section and the search ceilings are accepted, and study.md records each one as proven, empty or failed. The first witness is a gene-filtered aggregate request. The job reads the cBioPortal OpenAPI document retained in the 0194 evidence and names each request field that narrows a source gene aggregate reply, such as `/api/mutated-genes/fetch`, to named Entrez gene IDs. If such a field exists, the job sends that request once for `brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018` with Entrez 7157 and 3845 and once for `msk_impact_2017` with the same two IDs. If no such field exists, the job sends no aggregate request, and study.md records the absence beside the measured unfiltered costs of 4,258,105 bytes for BRCA and 113,843 bytes for MSK, for the PM to rule on. The second witness is an offline measurement of the whole 551-study catalog in the j14206 capture with no provider request: the largest single header, the largest five-header page over every consecutive window in source order, the largest description, and every distinct native `cancerTypeId` value with its study count. The third set is field witnesses: the catalog reply's revalidation headers, such as ETag and Last-Modified, read from the j14206 headers; one patient `projection=META&pageSize=1` request for `msk_impact_2017`, as a second known positive beside the BRCA total of 1084; and one DataHub GitHub contents request for a study directory that does not exist, as the files section's absent witness. Raw replies, headers, the ledger and the recipe go to the evidence store, and no product code changes.

- Why: the study ruling says the gene-count section must first prove a gene-filtered aggregate request because the unfiltered BRCA reply is 4.2 MB for one study, and study.md says to measure the whole 551-study catalog before accepting a search ceiling.
- Starts from: study.md's captured source findings, source-stated gene counts S21 to S26 and measured disclosure budget, with the 2026-10-26 study rulings, which win over the design text; the 0194 archives j14206, j14214 and j14223 on the evidence store and their manifests.
- Rules: the recipe names each exact URL and request body before the job runs, sends each row once in order with one second between admissions, a 20-second deadline and a 1 MiB body bound, and makes at most six physical requests. No retry, redirect, altered query, fallback host or extra page. A 401, 403 or 429 stops that source. No credential, cookie, institutional portal or archive download. The filtered witness passes only when the reply holds rows for the requested Entrez IDs alone and each row's `numberOfAlteredCases`, `numberOfAlteredCasesOnPanel`, `totalCount`, `numberOfProfiledCases` and panel IDs equal the unfiltered S21 to S24 rows. A field that filters samples instead of gene rows changes those counts and fails the witness. A reply over the body bound fails the witness, since the unfiltered BRCA reply exceeds it. A failed or empty filtered witness keeps the gene-count section blocked until the PM rules on the cost. No sample-list request runs, and no participant sample ID enters the ledger summary, study.md or the repo. No count is calculated from a capture.
- Proof: the job ledger records every attempt with its exact URL, body, status, bytes, SHA-256 and timing, and the archive path and hash appear in study.md beside each witness's result, and through pm progress. The offline catalog measurement records its recipe, input archive hash and output so a reviewer can reproduce it with no provider request. A reviewer checks that each witness's result names the capability it unlocks or blocks. No licensed content enters the repo, and replies whose terms bar storage replay from outside the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
