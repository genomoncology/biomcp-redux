## Source document findings

[NCBI access documentation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/docs/maintenance_use/) describes weekly comprehensive bulk reports and daily website updates. Its Entrez summary truncates condition display, so it cannot establish the complete condition set. [GTR overview](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gtr/docs/overview/) describes submitter-provided data and says FDA review information is not independently verified by NIH or FDA. A laboratory listing, test status and CLIA number do not establish device authorization. The inspected legacy parser's two reports are a limited tier, not the complete XML dataset.

[openFDA PMA](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/pma/) and [510(k)](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/510k/) document monthly updates. Their native identity and regulatory routes remain distinct. The PMA [field reference](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/pma/searchable-fields/) offers application/supplement, decision dates and approval-order statement; it does not document structured drug, gene, specimen or tumor linkage. The official [companion table](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/in-vitro-diagnostics/list-fda-authorized-companion-diagnostic-devices-in-vitro-and-imaging-tools) has those source columns and exact application/supplement and NDA/BLA identifiers. A candidate read-only table bundle is needed to answer companion questions without a name overlay. Do not infer those columns by mining approval-order prose.

The [WHO list](https://extranet.who.int/prequal/vitro-diagnostics/prequalified/in-vitro-diagnostics) publishes product IDs, multiple product codes, assay formats, manufacturer, marker, regulatory version and prequalification year. It includes HPV assays. A year is not an exact prequalification date; regulatory-version prose does not supply an FDA application ID. The site's list count is a web display observation, not a captured complete CSV count. The list gives no documented fixed update cadence; measure response metadata and disclose an unknown cadence.
