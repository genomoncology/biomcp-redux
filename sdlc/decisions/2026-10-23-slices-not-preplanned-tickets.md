# Slices, not preplanned tickets

Decided 2026-10-23 by Ian.

- Work starts with the architecture and a layered design, then proceeds in vertical slices.
- A slice is one entity with its sources. Simpler entities come first, building up to complex ones. Gene comes first, then articles.
- Tickets come out of each slice's investigation, never from a list made up front. No ticket is written for a later slice.
- `plan.md` names stages. `planning/slices.md` holds the method.
