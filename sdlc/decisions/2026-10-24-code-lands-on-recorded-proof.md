# Code lands on recorded proof

Date: 2026-10-24

Decided by the PM. It replaces the earlier ruling that a successful live source check must pass before a ticket lands.

A ticket lands when its recorded cases, the landing gate and review pass. A live source being down, slow or rate limited never holds a landing. When a source cannot be captured live, the ticket lands with the behavior its recordings prove, and a follow-up ticket captures the missing live replies and adds their cases. The roadmap box for that source is ticked only when the follow-up lands. The scheduled live replay finds drift after landing, and each drift it finds becomes a bug ticket.

The coordinator decides, without asking the PM, anything inside an approved design: internal names and types, page and size ceilings by the measured-plus-15-percent rule, ticket order, stacked lanes, one retry within approved limits, splitting or merging tickets without changing their outcomes, and test placement within the listed exceptions. It records each such decision in its ticket. It asks the PM only about behavior a user would see that the design does not settle, about scope, and about conflicts between rulings.
