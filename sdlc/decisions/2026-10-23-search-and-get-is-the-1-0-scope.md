# Search and get is the 1.0 scope

Decided 2026-10-23 by Ian.

- 1.0 covers search and get for every entity BioMCP 0.9 serves.
- A get may return a study. Article get includes supplementary material. Neither needs to become a biodata entity to be returned.
- Study analysis, charts and similar features are out of scope for now.
- The 1.0 bar is parity with 0.9's search and get, as `planning/milestones.md` says.
