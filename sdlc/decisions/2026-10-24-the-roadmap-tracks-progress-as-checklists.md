# The roadmap tracks progress as checklists

Date: 2026-10-24

Decided by Ian.

`ROADMAP.md` lists every biodata entity, every source and the foundations as checklists, plus a count of 0.9 parity, to show overall progress. This is a deliberate exception to keeping status only in pm. To keep the two from drifting, a box is ticked only in the commit that lands the work it names, and pm's tickets remain the record of work in flight.
