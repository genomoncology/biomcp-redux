# Eligibility text is source text

Date: 2026-10-27

Decided by the PM.

BioMCP retrieves a trial's registry eligibility text whole and in order, including cohort alternatives, exclusions, exceptions and time windows, and pages it without splitting a criterion (0317). 1.0 does not split or classify it. Search output keeps which query and fields found each trial, so a screen can show how each candidate matched.
