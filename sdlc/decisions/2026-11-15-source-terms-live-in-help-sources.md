# Source terms live in help sources

Date: 2026-11-15

Decided by Ian, in chat with the PM: "yes to both", answering the PM's two recommendations for 0849 (lean reply envelope).

## Decision

1. Each reply names every source and its `Use` label (`open`, `attribution`, `noncommercial`, `restricted`). The terms name and publisher page for every source live in `biomcp help sources` and `biomcp health`, not on each reply. This amends the "in the output" wording of `2026-10-24-sources-are-open-by-default.md` and `2026-10-26-new-sources-follow-open-by-default.md`.
2. Orphadata replies keep the statement Orphanet's legal notice asks an AI tool to carry (that results may not reflect Orphanet/Orphadata data and Orphanet is not responsible for them), on Orphadata results only. 0889 first put it on every Orphadata reply (`sdlc/tickets/0889-orphanet-prevalence-on-disease-cards.md:44`).

## Why

- 0849 measured ten recorded replies: dropping `terms` and `terms_url` from each source saves 7.5% overall (3.5% to 14.7% per reply), and every reply is re-read each turn (`sdlc/records/2026-11-11-token-gap-root-cause.md`).
- The 0849 review found Orphanet's notice the one licence that asks for a statement to accompany results; the CC BY, CC BY-SA and ODbL sources allow attribution "in any reasonable manner", which the source name, `Use` label and `help sources` give.
