## Writing tickets

When a ticket relies on an outside boundary, such as a dependency's behavior or a source's vocabulary, its Starts from or Proof names the exact capability or vocabulary someone checked, with a minimal example, and lists any assumption still unchecked. A citation alone is not proof of the boundary.
