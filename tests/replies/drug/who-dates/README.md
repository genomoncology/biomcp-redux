# WHO PQ date ranges: synthetic export

WHO PQ is a noncommercial source, so this replay holds no real WHO rows (0695). The 657-row export keeps the real export's columns, quoting, blank cells and padded date format, with every reference, name, applicant and category invented. In 0910 each date moved to an invented day in the same month that no row of WHO's export of 2026-11-13 uses, keeping year and month windows, order and ties, and each `10mg` strength became `17mg`.
