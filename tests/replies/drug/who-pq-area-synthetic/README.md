# WHO PQ therapeutic area: synthetic export

WHO PQ is a noncommercial source, so this replay holds no real WHO rows (0695, 0898). The eight rows are invented: every reference, medicine name, applicant, strength and date. Only the therapeutic area names (Oncology, HIV/AIDS, Tuberculosis, Hepatitis B and C, Malaria) are WHO's own category labels, because the area filter matches them. One oncology row has no date, so the case shows undated rows last. Checked in 0910: no row's date, or date and strength, appears in WHO's export of 2026-11-13.
